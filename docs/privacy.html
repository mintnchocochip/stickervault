Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026 · Applies to the StickerVault Android app and this website

StickerVault collects nothing. No personal data, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting. The app declares no internet permission, so the operating system itself prevents it from transmitting anything anywhere — this is not a promise about our intentions, it is a restriction Android enforces on the app.

What the app can access

StickerVault only reads a folder you choose yourself, through Android's system folder picker. It cannot browse your storage on its own.

  • The WhatsApp sticker folder, once you select it and grant access. Used to find the .webp sticker files to archive.
  • A vault zip you select when restoring, so its contents can be extracted.

Access is a grant you give and can withdraw at any time in Android's settings. The app never reads your messages, contacts, photos, location, or any other app's data — it has no permission to do so.

What the app stores, and where

WhatWhere
The archive zip Your device's Downloads folder. Yours to move, copy or delete.
Imported stickers and their index The app's private storage on your device.
Sticker packs offered to WhatsApp The app's private storage, read by WhatsApp on your device.
Your folder choice App preferences, so you are not asked again.

All of it stays on your device. None of it is uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. There is no server, no account, and no sign-in.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anybody. If you use the share button to send your archive somewhere — Google Drive, a messaging app, a computer — that is an action you take, using the app you chose, and that app's own privacy policy applies to what happens next. StickerVault simply hands the file to Android.

Permissions, and why each exists

PermissionReason
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC		 Archiving thousands of stickers takes minutes. This lets the work continue when you leave the app rather than being killed halfway.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show archiving progress and tell you when it has finished. Optional — declining it does not stop the app working.

There is deliberately no storage permission and no INTERNET permission. You can verify this yourself in the app's manifest, which is public source code.

Children

StickerVault is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone regardless of age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, including imported stickers and prepared packs. Archives you saved to Downloads or copied elsewhere are yours and are not touched by uninstalling. Within the app, "Delete imported library" clears the imported stickers immediately.

Note that uninstalling also removes any sticker packs the app supplied to WhatsApp, because Android WhatsApp reads third-party packs from the app that provides them rather than copying them in.

This website

These pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. No cookies are set and no analytics are used. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving them, as it would for any website using that service. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Since the app collects no data, no change can retroactively affect information already gathered — there is none.
